GSC

          
A budget planner for a day, a week or a month. List what you need to buy with prices, tick items off as you buy them, and GSC shows what's left and how much you can spend each day. Everything is saved on your device.

          
JavaScriptVitelocalStorageNetlify

          

            Project details
            

              
The problem

              
A short-term budget means doing the maths every time you shop: what's already spent, what's still on the list, and how much is left for each remaining day.

              
What it does

              
    
                
  • Set a budget for a day, a week, a month or a set number of days
    • 
                
  • Add items with quantity and price; GSC totals the list and checks that it fits
    • 
                
  • Tick items off as you buy them and log any other spending
    • 
                
  • Shows what's left and a daily spending amount that updates by itself
    • 
                
  • Data stays on the device, so there's no account to create
    •

LUMEN

          
An achievement jar for every win, big or tiny. Each win you log drops into the jar as a star, and a weekly tracker, all-time stats and a daily quote keep you going. Everything is saved in your browser, so there's no sign-up.

          
HTML/CSSJavaScriptlocalStorageNetlify

          

            Project details
            

              
The problem

              
Small wins are easy to forget. LUMEN gives them a place to land, so progress stays visible on the days it doesn't feel like it.

              
What it does

              
    
                
  • Add a win and it drops into the jar as a star
    • 
                
  • Weekly tracker with wins per day and a 7-day streak bar
    • 
                
  • All-time and best-day stats
    • 
                
  • A daily quote, with a button to pick another
    • 
                
  • The Log: a list of every past win
    • 
                
  • Personal greeting; everything is saved in the browser
